Creator Outreach & Onboarding Industry Benchmarks

• Below 20%: Outreach & Friction Bottleneck

Industry data (Modash & Tapfiliate) shows cold creator response averages 5%–15%. If tapped-to-onboarded is below 20%, creators are dropping off due to high sign-up friction, missing commission clarity, or lack of 24–48h follow-up sequences.

• 20% – 35%: Healthy Seeding & Affiliate Baseline

Established benchmark for personalized creator seeding and warm affiliate invitations (typically 20%–35% on nano/micro-influencers). Indicates strong value proposition, swift reply times, and clear compensation structures.

• 35%+: High-Yield Conversion

Top-quartile creator program performance (often seen in highly aligned product gifting with structured 72-hour onboarding frameworks). Feed this exact acceptance percentage into the Creator Seeding & Target Tracker to calculate daily onboarding requirements.
